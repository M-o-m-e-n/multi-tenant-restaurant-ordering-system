Restaurant Ordering System — 31 MVP Design Directions

Each file below is a self-contained, interactive mockup of the customer menu screen (the screen that defines the design system for POS, KDS and Admin too). Try the category filters and “+” buttons inside each preview — open any design full-screen for the real feel.

Static HTML/CSS prototypes — the final pick gets rebuilt in React + Tailwind. Every design serves the same nine dishes at the same prices, so you are comparing design and not menu copy.

How to choose — score each design 1–5 on:

  • Readability at arm's length — prices & names instantly scannable?
  • Photo appetite appeal — does real food photography fit the style?
  • Brand fit — matches the restaurant concept you imagine?
  • Cart clarity — is the order/total always obvious?
  • Extends to POS/KDS — can staff screens inherit this style?
  • Day & night — light or dark better for your venue lighting?
  • Accessibility — contrast & tap targets feel comfortable?
  • Build effort — any style that's expensive to implement?
  • Interaction model — list, stepper, chat or map? Round two changes this, not just the skin.
  • Service style — does it assume table service, a counter queue, or locker pickup?
  • Scales past nine dishes — still works at a 60-item menu?
